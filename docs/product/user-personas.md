# Serller — User Personas

> Status: Complete (Phase 1)

## Persona 1 — "Stella", the Stellar native (primary)

- **Age:** 28 · **Role:** Freighter wallet user, Soroban dApp explorer.
- **Behavior:** Already holds XLM; reads Stellar ecosystem news; tries new testnet dApps; hates seed-phrase forms.
- **Pain points:** Most "Web3 social" apps are Ethereum-centric with gas fees; nothing on Stellar feels social.
- **Goal:** Find a place where her wallet *is* her identity, and tip creators she likes with XLM in one tap.
- **Serller fit:** wallet connect → authenticated instantly → posts + tips. She becomes an ambassador.

## Persona 2 — "Marc", the creator

- **Age:** 34 · **Role:** Writer/artist with a small but loyal audience.
- **Behavior:** Posts daily content; currently monetizes via Patreon (30% + platform lock-in).
- **Pain points:** High platform fees, opaque algorithms, fear of deplatforming.
- **Goal:** Direct, near-free monetization; verifiable ownership of his content.
- **Serller fit:** posts with images on IPFS, notarizes key posts on-chain (proof of authorship), receives XLM tips directly.

## Persona 3 — "Dee", the crypto-curious social user

- **Age:** 22 · **Role:** College student, active on mainstream social.
- **Behavior:** Follows tech/creator accounts; has heard of "crypto" but owns nothing.
- **Pain points:** Intimidated by wallets and gas; wants simplicity.
- **Goal:** A social app that feels normal, with a fun optional crypto layer.
- **Serller fit:** browses feed without a wallet; when ready, installs Freighter, connects on Testnet (friendbot funds), tips 1 XLM, sees it settle in seconds. Onboarding is gentle.

## Persona 4 — "Vik", the testnet explorer

- **Age:** 31 · **Role:** Developer evaluating Stellar.
- **Behavior:** Runs everything on Testnet, reads contracts, checks explorer links.
- **Goal:** Test Serller end-to-end on Testnet, inspect the notary contract, reuse patterns.
- **Serller fit:** full Testnet support, explorer links on every tip, open-source contracts with tests.

## Non-goals (explicitly NOT Serller personas)

- People seeking speculative trading/gambling — Serller has no exchange, no portfolio P&L, no yield.
- Enterprise users — no B2B features in MVP.
